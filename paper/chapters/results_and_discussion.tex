\section{Results and Discussion}
Testing was conducted over the four previously datasets. There were a total
of 2045 words evaluated. The model achieved an overall CER of 11.33\%, but
applying different CER variants improved the performance. The variant that
treats \{e, i, y\} characters as equivalent achieved a CER of 8.76\%, while
the variant that treats \{o, u\} as equivalent achieved a CER of 11.20\%.
Combining both variants results improved the results even further, resulting
in a CER of 8.63\%.
