\section{Results and Discussion}
As a testbed we implemented orthographic nativization of English words in
Filipino.~\Cref{CER} shows the CERs for default case, e/i/y relaxed, o/u

\begin{table}[h]
    \centering

    \caption{Performance (Character Error Rate)}
    \label{CER}
    \begin{tabular}{|l|c|c|c|c|}
        \hline
            & Default & e = i = y & o = u   & Combined \\
        \hline
        CER & 11.33\% & 8.76\%    & 11.20\% & 8.63\%   \\
        \hline
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Testing was conducted over the four previously mentioned datasets. There were a
total of 2,045 words evaluated.

The base model achieved an overall CER of 11.33\%. For the methodology, refer
to the last paragraph of Experimental Setup. The results show that treating
\{e, i, y\} as equivalent reduced the CER from 11.33\% to 8.76\%, showing that
this variant is what most of the improvement consists of. Whereas the \{o, u\}
variant only reduced the CER by 0.13\%, from 11.33\% to 11.20\%. Combining both
variants resulted in the lowest CER, with 8.63\%.
