\section{Results and Discussion}
Testing over the four datasets, there were a total of 2045 words
evaluated. There was an overall CER of 11.33\%. With the variant that
treats \{e, i, y\} characters as equivalent, there a CER of 8.76\%.
With the variant that treats \{o, u\} as equivalent, there was a CER
of 11.20\%. Combining both variants results in a CER of 8.63\%.
