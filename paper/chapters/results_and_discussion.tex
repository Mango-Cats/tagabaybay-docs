\section{Results and Discussion}
As a testbench we implemented orthigraphic nativization of English words
in Filipino. \ref{CER} shows the CERs for default case, e/i/y relaxed, o/u
relaxed, and combined.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering

\caption{Performance (Character Error Rate)}
\label{CER}
\begin{tabular}{|l|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
    & Default & e = i = y & o = u   & Combined \\
\hline
CER & 11.33\% & 8.76\%    & 11.20\% & 8.63\% \\
\hline

\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Testing was conducted over the four previously mentioned datasets. There were
a total of 2,045 words evaluated. The model achieved an overall CER of 11.33\%,
but applying different CER variants improved the performance. The variant that
treats \{e, i, y\} characters as equivalent achieved a CER of 8.76\%, while
the variant that treats \{o, u\} as equivalent achieved a CER of 11.20\%.
Combining both variants improved the results even further, resulting in a CER
of 8.63\%.
