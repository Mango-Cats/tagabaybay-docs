\section{Conclusion}

We presented a rule-based rewrite cascade for orthographic nativization of
English loanwords into Filipino. The system achieved 5.14\% CER under strict
evaluation and 4.05\% under allographic vowel equivalence, without parallel
training data. Error analysis identifies schwa-derived vowel ambiguity as the
primary remaining error source. Incorporation of lexical stress information or
a hybrid rule-and-data approach could yield further gains. The framework's
transparency makes it suitable for low-resource scenarios where annotated
loanword corpora are unavailable. Future work will extend the approach to other
language pairs and evaluate on informal register text.
