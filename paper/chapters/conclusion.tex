\section{Conclusion}

We presented a rule-based cascade for orthographic nativization of English
loanwords into Filipino, demonstrating that prescriptive linguistic rules can
be operationalized into an effective computational system without parallel
training data. On X loanwords, the proposed method achieved X\% CER
under strict evaluation and X\% when accounting for Filipino's allographic
vowel variation.

Error analysis reveals that most remaining errors stem from schwa ambiguity in
unstressed syllables, a limitation that could be addressed through
prosody-aware G2P or data-driven refinement of vowel mappings. The framework's
transparency and linguistic grounding make it suitable for low-resource
scenarios where annotated loanword corpora are unavailable.
