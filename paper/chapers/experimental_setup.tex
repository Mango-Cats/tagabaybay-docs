\section{Experimental Setup}

\subsection{Implementation}

As mentioned previously, we will use Filipino as a testbench for
this study

\subsection{Gold Standard}
The gold standard was manually constructed with 1,582 instances, consisting of
English words alongside their adapted versions. This gold standard composed
of English words commonly being used in Filipino-contexts or in code
switching.
 
We then validated the accuracy of the gold standard via inter-annotator
agreement and cross-validating the annotations with Filipino orthography.

\subsection{Evaluation}

The implementation was evaluated on the gold standard using Character Error
Rate (CER) which is the error on a per-character basis, calculated
as the Levenshein distance between the expected and actual output.
